# Asset provenance

## Gameplay art
All game geometry, surface patterns, signs and rickshaw panel motifs are generated by original project code. No commercial game assets, ripped models, celebrity likenesses or third-party character designs are used.

The setting is an artistic compression inspired by Old Dhaka. Reference sources:
- UNESCO, Rickshaws and rickshaw painting in Dhaka: https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/rickshaws-and-rickshaw-painting-in-dhaka-01589
- Bangladesh Tourism Board, Ahsan Manzil: https://beautifulbangladesh.gov.bd/loc/dhaka/3
- Bangladesh Tourism Board, Dhaka visitor guide: https://beautifulbangladesh.gov.bd/frontend/assets/pdf/Dhaka.pdf

## Title concept
`public/art/cover.png` and `docs/references/concept-v1.png` were generated with OpenAI ImageGen for this project. The menu explicitly labels this as concept art. The in-game scene is independently constructed 3D geometry.

Creative brief: cinematic golden-hour Old Dhaka lane, a rear-view cyclist in an ochre shirt on a teal bicycle, hand-painted rickshaw at left, teal bus at right, layered historic shopfronts with Bangla signs and striped awnings, tangled wires, warm weathered plaster, lively street details, and pink Ahsan Manzil across the distant opening. Teal, ochre, terracotta and cream palette; no HUD in the source art. Composition reserves space for the game's title and controls.

### Traffic field recording

- Source: [City traffic sound](https://freesound.org/people/Balaram_Mahalder/sounds/715399/) by **Balaram_Mahalder**, published December 14, 2023.
- License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), verified on the source page September 11, 2026. Courtesy credit is included in the game's sound credits.
- Public HQ preview used: `https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/715/715399_15504679-hq.mp3`, exposed by the source page. Downloaded preview is 61.248 seconds, stereo, 48 kHz. The original upload is listed as M4A; we processed the MP3 preview.
- Location caveat: description names Dhaka, while the HTML explicitly labels the geotag as Muladi, Barisal, Bangladesh. Exact location is unverified. Do not call this a verified Shankhari Bazaar, Sadarghat or Karwan Bazar recording.
- Shipped asset: `public/audio/dhaka-traffic-loop.mp3`; 38 seconds, stereo, 32 kHz, 128 kbps, 609,452 bytes. Decoded at 32 kHz it occupies approximately 9.7 MB; a browser decoding at 48 kHz uses approximately 14.6 MB.
- Processing: source seconds 4–44, high-pass 80 Hz, low-pass 6.5 kHz, loudness normalization targeting −22 LUFS / −3 dBTP / 9 LU LRA, then a two-second end-to-start crossfade. Output is encoded as gapless MP3. Measured final MP3: −22.02 LUFS integrated, −7.01 dBTP, 6.10 LU loudness range (FFmpeg EBU R128/loudnorm analysis).
- The loop connects through the existing ambience mix so dialogue/rear warnings duck it. Playback stops on pause/mute/hidden/reset, resumes at its prior offset after pause, and restarts from zero for a new ride. Failed downloads retain synthesized ambience.
- Listening review of source content and the final in-game mix remains pending; codec, duration and playback lifecycle are checked separately.

Another candidate, [Dhaka Street Sound-Binaural by azdipu](https://freesound.org/people/azdipu/sounds/843558/), uses CC-BY-NC 4.0. It was not bundled, preserving the possibility of commercial release without that restriction.

V2 request identifiers: launch `b8dc8610-7a76-4df5-9ced-ab690710bb43`; vegetable vendor `42a9f827-7cfb-4c07-bbbb-39bd6a010aac`. Provider-reported durations were 2.717 and 1.411 seconds respectively; listening review is still required for completeness and delivery. Captions convey the call's meaning rather than claiming word-perfect transcription.

Additional district references: [Banglapedia: Shankhari](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Shankhari), [Banglapedia: Kawran Bazar](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Kawran_Bazar), and [Bangladesh Tourism Board: Sadarghat](https://www.beautifulbangladesh.gov.bd/index.php/district-destination/dhaka/landmarks/115). These informed neighborhood motifs, not an exact street map.

## Fonts
The interface requests Barlow Condensed, DM Sans and Noto Sans Bengali through Google Fonts, with local system fallbacks. The game remains playable when that request fails; Bengali sign appearance depends on available font support. Fonts are not yet bundled for offline use.

## Sound and music, drop 2b (2026-10-12)
- **Effects, bells, horns and district soundscapes** (`src/soundbank.ts`, `src/soundscape.ts`) are original Web Audio synthesis written for this project. There are no samples. The shapes imitate Dhaka street sounds: poisha coins, tea glasses, the jhalmuri seller's tin, conch-shell workshop sawing, launch horns and the ice-cream cart bell. No religious or devotional sounds are used.
- **Adaptive music** (`src/music.ts`) is an original arrangement synthesized in the browser: dhol, ektara, harmonium and bansuri voices on a 96 BPM grid. The tune is original.
- **Generated district beds** (Eleven Music) are supported by `src/musicplayer.ts` and `public/audio/music/manifest.json`, but none have shipped yet. When they do, `scripts/loop-music.py` tempo-locks each to 96 BPM, cuts a 16-bar (40 s) loop with 30 ms fades, and levels it to −20 LUFS at 96 kbps. Each one will be recorded here with its prompt, date and plan terms.
- **Street voices:** 17 lines are listed in `docs/voice-script.md`. Five use the existing HeyGen placeholders described above. The other twelve are caption-only until the family records them (`npm run add-voice`) or AI placeholders are generated.
