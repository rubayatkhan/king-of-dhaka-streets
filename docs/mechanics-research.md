# Mechanics research and Dhaka adaptations

Research checked September 11, 2026. These primary sources document mechanics and design choices in established games. They do not prove that a borrowed element will make this game viral, addictive or enjoyable. The adaptations below are design hypotheses to test with players.

| Reference | Documented idea | Implemented Dhaka adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Traffic Rider — developer](https://www.skgames.com/traffic-rider.html) | Career missions, progression and bonuses for close overtakes. | Fifteen short missions, three per vehicle; optional near-miss, health and time targets for gold. Bicycle missions begin with reading traffic gaps and graduate to reactive danger. |
| [Subway Surfers — official mission help](https://sybo.helpshift.com/hc/en/5-subway-surfers/faq/143-missions/?p=all) | Sets of missions give repeat runs objectives and progression. | Visible three-mission chapter cards, persistent medals and personal bests. Repeating a completed mission improves its result but cannot skip a locked mission. |
| [Crossy Road — official Dino World update](https://www.crossyroad.com/updates/dino-world-makeover) | Clear hazard boundaries, directional warnings and consistent movement improve readability. | A rear attacker warns for three seconds and commits to its line; paan has a windup, fixed landing target, flight and splash. Rear launches postpone or cancel if the tested escape space is blocked. |
| [Crazy Taxi — SEGA manual, pp. 3–4](https://shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/71230/manuals/DC_CT_PC_MG_EFIGS_FINAL_lr.pdf?t=1733765150) | Destination jobs, time pressure and skillful-driving tips. | Local trip framing—bakarkhani breakfast, catching a launch, wedding invitations, market cargo—plus close-shave chains and optional time targets. These are currently route-completion missions, not passenger pickup or cargo-delivery simulations. |

The core loop is: read a gap → commit or brake → narrowly escape → receive an immediate Bangla reaction and score → approach a new street problem. Ordinary clean passes remain lower-value; close passes build a chain that expires after five seconds. Damage breaks the chain, so near misses are a choice with a cost.

The distinctive mechanic is Dhaka's changing traffic hierarchy. A bicycle must navigate the opening CNG, rickshaw and bus. A truck makes those same actors pull farther aside before contact. Each upgrade changes size, handling, camera, engine, horn and deference, not just a badge. Cows and potholes remain physical obstacles.

Difficulty increases within each vehicle chapter. First lessons introduce static hazards and ordinary traffic; second lessons add a rear threat and paan throw; mastery lessons combine more reactive encounters. Seeded variation supplies repeatability without requiring a new map each retry. Rear attacks use reachable-space checks and breathing room between reactive events.

The three scenery families are inspired by Shankhari Bazaar's heritage shops, Sadarghat's launches and riverfront, and Karwan Bazar's wholesale commerce and larger buildings. Art and sound change together. The route compresses these places fictionally rather than presenting a geographic reconstruction. ## What player tests must decide

- Can a new player understand steering, braking, the rear warning and paan landing area without explanation?
- Does failure feel attributable to a decision, and does retry invite a different approach?
- Are three bicycle missions satisfying preparation, or repetitive delay?
- Do gold targets encourage skillful risk without making ordinary finishes feel inadequate?
- Is the stronger traffic deference obvious immediately after an upgrade?
- Do Dhaka residents recognize the places, street calls and humor, and do the voices sound natural?

Automated pilots establish that tested routes are traversable. They cannot establish human fun, cultural authenticity, retention or physical-phone performance.
