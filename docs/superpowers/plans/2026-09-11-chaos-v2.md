# Dhaka Chaos v2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans task by task; parallel ownership is explicit below.

**Goal:** Deliver the user's requested expansion with changing Dhaka environments, rear dangers, paan throws, cows, stronger audio and multi-stage vehicle progression.
**Architecture:** Pure campaign/simulation, world rendering, audio and UI remain separate. Root integrates progression and presentation while three workers own independent modules.
**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Three.js, Vite, Vitest.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-11-chaos-v2-design.md

## Global constraints
- Mobile browser, chase camera, current touch/keyboard controls.
- Fifteen missions; three distinct completions per vehicle before graduation.
- At least three seconds warning for rear threats and reachable escape space.
- Keep ROAD_HALF_WIDTH=5.3; environmental setbacks may differ.
- No native-phone or AAA claims based solely on screenshots or automated tests.
