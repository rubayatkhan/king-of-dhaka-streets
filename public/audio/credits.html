The sound of the street.

Our background traffic uses “City traffic sound” by Balaram_Mahalder, shared under CC0 1.0.

The creator describes the recording as Dhaka traffic. Its geotag points to Muladi, Barisal, so the exact location is unverified. It is used as general ambience, without claiming to document any particular in-game district.

We trimmed, filtered, adjusted loudness and crossfaded the recording into a 38-second loop. It becomes quieter for dialogue and danger warnings.

Vehicle effects, bells, warnings and additional environmental textures are original synthesis. The Bangla street voices are AI placeholders made with ElevenLabs (two of the voices were designed for this game) until our family recordings replace them. They are not recordings of Dhaka residents.
